Try the physical-control view

  1. Bus = 0101. Hold LOAD A. Data follows bus→MUX→A; LOAD_A comes into A from below.
  2. Release LOAD A. One rising edge latches A.
  3. Bus = 0011. Hold LOAD B. The bus enters B from the side and LOAD_B enters from below.
  4. Hold ADD. A and B feed the adder, SUM returns to the second MUX input, SEL_A comes from below, and LOAD_A prepares A to latch the result.
