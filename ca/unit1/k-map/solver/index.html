5 · Logic circuit for the minimized expression

For SOP, each product term is formed by an AND gate. The product terms are then combined by an OR gate. Required complemented inputs pass through NOT gates.

Reading the circuit: a bar/prime means the input is complemented. A one-literal term can connect directly to the final OR gate. If the minimized result has only one term, no final OR gate is required.
